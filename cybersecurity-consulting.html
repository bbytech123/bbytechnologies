Cybersecurity Consulting

Clear, practical guidance to help you understand security risks, make better technology decisions, and strengthen the systems your business depends on.

Home› Services› Cybersecurity› Consulting

Areas I can help with

Security guidance across your technology environment.

Consulting can focus on one specific concern or review several areas together to identify practical improvements and priorities.

Website & Domain Security

Review the security of your website, domain, DNS, email authentication, and the accounts used to manage your online presence.

  • Domain and registrar security
  • DNS, HTTPS, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC guidance
  • Website access and administrator review

Network Security

Evaluate how your network is designed and whether devices, users, and services are separated and protected appropriately.

  • Firewall and network architecture review
  • VLAN and guest network segmentation
  • Remote access and VPN recommendations

Identity & Account Security

Strengthen how users sign in and reduce the risk created by weak passwords, excessive permissions, and unsecured administrator accounts.

  • MFA and sign-in protection
  • Administrator and permission review
  • Account lifecycle recommendations

Cloud & Microsoft 365 Security

Review cloud settings and business collaboration tools to help protect email, files, identities, and shared company data.

  • Microsoft 365 security review
  • Sharing and access recommendations
  • Email and cloud data protection guidance

Endpoint & Device Security

Review the computers, mobile devices, and other endpoints that access business systems and recommend practical ways to reduce risk.

  • Device security and update practices
  • Encryption and endpoint protection
  • Business and personal device separation

Security Planning & Recommendations

Turn technical findings into a clear plan so you know what matters most, what can wait, and where your budget will have the greatest impact.

  • Risk and priority review
  • Technology and vendor recommendations
  • Practical security improvement roadmap

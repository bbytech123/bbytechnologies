Firewalls & Network Protection

Your firewall is the first line of defense between your business and the public internet. A properly configured firewall can block malicious traffic, restrict risky connections, and reduce exposure to attackers ranging from automated bots and crypto thieves to organized cybercriminals and nation-state activity.

  • Firewall installation, replacement, and configuration review
  • Threat blocking, intrusion prevention, and geo-based controls
  • Secure VPN access and network segmentation

Identity & Account Protection

Many attacks start with a stolen password rather than a broken firewall. Strengthening how users sign in can make compromised credentials far less useful to an attacker.

  • Multi-factor authentication and stronger sign-in policies
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace account protection
  • Admin account separation and least-privilege access

Email & Cloud Security

Email and cloud accounts hold some of your most valuable business information. Security controls can reduce phishing, malicious links, fraudulent sign-ins, and accidental exposure of sensitive data.

  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace security settings

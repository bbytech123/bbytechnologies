About BBY Technologies

Braxton Barley

Founder · Engineer · Austin, Texas

My passion is helping people and organizations solve problems, expand their knowledge, and keep their resources secure. I created BBY Technologies in 2026 to do this primarily through computer systems.

I was born and raised in the Lake Travis area of Austin, Texas, before attending Texas A&M to study IT, cybersecurity, and business. I moved back to Austin a few years ago to embrace the tech hub. Now, I help local small and medium-sized businesses solve problems and stay safe.

One of BBY’s main focuses is restaurants and venues. I’ve worked in several throughout my life and have always admired the hands-on, hardworking people who make service a core part of their life. Early on, I created BBY Restaurants as a pillar of the business to make solving technology problems simpler and more efficient for some of the busiest people I’ve ever met.

At BBY, we believe that information and tools should be open to all. A rising tide lifts all boats, so please check out our completely free guides and tools here.
